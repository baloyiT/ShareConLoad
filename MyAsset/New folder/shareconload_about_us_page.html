The problem

Global trade is broken for everyone who isn't a large corporation

The international freight industry runs on relationships, WhatsApp messages, and guesswork. Operators haul empty space at their own cost. Shippers overpay for space they don't need. Agents manage fragmented chaos across dozens of conversations. And small and medium businesses across the world remain locked out of international trade entirely — because the system was never built for them.

These aren't isolated frustrations. They are structural failures that cost the global trade ecosystem billions every year. We mapped every one of them and built the solution.

Empty space = lost revenue

Operators lose money every time a container sails with unfilled space. There was no marketplace to fix it — until now.

Shippers overpay

Paying full FCL rates for partial loads is the norm. No platform existed to connect shippers to the space that's already there.

Agents work blind

Freight forwarders and clearing agents juggle operators and shippers across email, phone, and WhatsApp — with no single system of record.

No trust layer

Cross-border transactions between unknown parties move on faith. Money is sent. Goods don't always follow. There is no escrow, no protection, no recourse.

Information asymmetry

Operators don't know who needs space. Shippers don't know who has it. The global LCL market is enormous — and completely dark.

SMEs locked out

Small businesses in emerging markets cannot access international trade affordably. Full container costs are a wall they can't climb alone.

Share the Load, Connect the World

We built the marketplace that global trade was missing

ShareConLoad is a live digital container load-sharing marketplace — the first platform purpose-built to connect Operators, Shippers, and Agents in one verified, payments-protected ecosystem.

The solution

One platform. Three powerful roles. Zero wasted space.

ShareConLoad is a two-sided marketplace with three distinct user types — each solving a different piece of the same broken puzzle. Every role benefits directly from the others being on the platform.

Operator

You have the space

List your available container space, set your rates, and connect with verified shippers on your trade corridor. Stop sailing empty. Start earning on every cubic metre.

Shipper

You need the space

Book only what you need. No full container costs, no middleman markups, no payment risk. Shippers pay zero platform fees — your goods move, your margins stay intact.

Agent

You connect both worlds

Freight forwarders and clearing agents operate on both sides of the marketplace. One platform replaces the chaos of dozens of conversations — and your commission is structured, transparent, and protected.

Why ShareConLoad

Not just a platform — a complete trade infrastructure

We have built more than a booking tool. ShareConLoad is a fully integrated ecosystem that handles discovery, matching, payment, and settlement — end to end. Payments are processed through Paystack and Flutterwave with MTN Mobile Money integration, making the platform genuinely accessible across Africa and beyond. Escrow is milestone-based: funds are released in stages tied to real shipment progress — so every party is protected at every step.

Our commission structure is tiered and decreasing as volume grows, rewarding operators and agents who scale with the platform. Shippers always pay zero platform fees — because removing friction for cargo owners is how we grow the supply side. This is a marketplace built with commercial intelligence, not just good intentions.
