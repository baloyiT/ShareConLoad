# Draft Reply — Paystack Review (Grace Nnabugwu)

Hi Grace,

Thank you for the follow-up. Please find detailed responses to each of your questions below, with links to the relevant public pages on our website.

---

**1. How are payments handled?**

ShareConLoad operates as a digital logistics marketplace. All customer payments are processed
exclusively via Paystack. Our payment architecture works as follows:

- A customer books container space on the platform and pays a **20% deposit** via a Paystack
  payment link generated by our Supabase Edge Function (`initialize-payment`). The customer
  is redirected to Paystack's hosted payment page. No card data ever touches our servers.
- Paystack processes the transaction and sends a webhook to our `paystack-webhook` Edge
  Function, which verifies the signature and records the payment as `paid`.
- The remaining balance is collected in two further stages:
  - **50% pre-departure payment** — due 7 days before the container departs, triggered by
    a milestone update from the operator.
  - **30% final release payment** — due at destination, required before cargo is released.
- All three stages use the same Paystack-hosted flow.
- Operator **payouts** are executed via Paystack Transfer API from our Edge Function
  (`trigger-payout`). Payouts are only released once: (a) the corresponding customer payment
  stage is confirmed as `paid`, (b) the operator has a verified Paystack recipient code,
  (c) no active dispute exists on the booking, and (d) the 48-hour refund window has elapsed
  for Stage 1.
- ShareConLoad deducts a **tiered commission** (6–12%) from each payout stage before transfer.
- Paystack secret keys are stored only in Supabase Edge Function secrets — never in frontend
  environment variables.

Full details: https://shareconload.com/payment-flow

---

**2. Vetting process for logistics providers (KYC)**

Operators (logistics providers) undergo a mandatory verification process before they can list
containers or receive payouts. The following documents are required:

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Proof of Identity | Valid passport or national ID of director/owner |
| Business Registration | Certificate of incorporation from country's business registry |
| Proof of Warehouse Address | Lease, rates account, or utility bill confirming warehouse location |
| Tax Clearance Certificate | Tax compliance certificate from revenue authority |
| Banking Confirmation | Official bank letter confirming account details |
| Cargo Insurance Certificate | Valid cargo/freight insurance policy |
| Freight Forwarding License | (Optional) Issued by relevant authority where applicable |

An administrator reviews each document and approves or rejects it individually. Operators
cannot receive payouts until all required documents are approved and their `payout_enabled`
flag is set by the admin. Listings can be created before KYC is complete, but payouts
are withheld until full verification.

Full details: https://shareconload.com/operator-verification

---

**3. Pricing structure**

ShareConLoad charges a **commission-based fee only** — there are no subscription fees,
listing fees, or monthly charges for operators.

Commission is tiered and deducted from each payout stage:

| Shipment Value | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| R0 – R5 000 | 12% |
| R5 001 – R20 000 | 10% |
| R20 001 – R50 000 | 8% |
| R50 001 and above | 6% |

Rates apply to the gross amount per payout stage, not the total booking value.

Shippers pay the price per CBM set by the operator — no additional platform fee is charged
to shippers beyond what the operator sets in their listing (operators may build their margin
into their listed price).

Full details: https://shareconload.com/pricing

---

**4. How disputes are handled**

Customers can raise a dispute against any confirmed, in-transit, or delivered shipment
through the platform's dispute portal. The process is:

1. **Submission** — Customer selects the booking, chooses a dispute type (cargo damage,
   short delivery, overcharge, unreasonable delay, or other), and provides a written description.
2. **Evidence** — The customer may upload supporting evidence (photos, documents).
3. **Hold** — Any pending payout to the operator for the disputed booking is automatically
   blocked until the dispute is resolved.
4. **Admin review** — Our operations team reviews the dispute within 2 business days,
   may request additional information, and determines a resolution.
5. **Resolution** — Outcomes may include: refund to customer, release of payout to operator,
   partial adjustment, platform credits, or operator penalty (trust-score reduction,
   suspension, or removal).

Full details: https://shareconload.com/dispute-resolution

---

**5. Refund and cancellation policy**

Our full policy is published at: https://shareconload.com/cancellation

Summary:
- **Within 48 hours of booking:** 20% deposit may be refunded (platform service fees non-refundable)
- **After 48 hours:** Deposit is non-refundable
- **After pre-departure payment (50%):** Partial refund subject to review; deposit non-refundable
- **After vessel departure:** Fully non-refundable
- **Refunds are never automatic** — each case is reviewed by the admin team
- **Paystack processes all refunds** via our `process-refund` Edge Function after admin approval

---

We have also added the above information to the following new public pages on our website
to ensure full transparency:

- Payment Flow: https://shareconload.com/payment-flow
- Operator Verification: https://shareconload.com/operator-verification
- Dispute Resolution: https://shareconload.com/dispute-resolution
- Pricing: https://shareconload.com/pricing
- Cancellation & Refund Policy: https://shareconload.com/cancellation

Please let us know if you need any further information or documentation.

Warm regards,
Justice Baloyi
VEYQON GROUP (Pty) Ltd
support@shareconload.com
